About SkillBridge

SkillBridge connects polytechnic and university students with working mentors. You can get quick answers on AI and career skills in one short chat, or join a group workshop and meet others.

Mentor chats happen at BizCampus Mentorship Corners in university outlets and at BizCampus pop-ups on the Singapore Polytechnic campus, so you do not need to travel far between classes.
